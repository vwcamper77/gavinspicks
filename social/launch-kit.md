## Ongoing listing post template

Hook: “[Year] [model] — £[asking price]”
Details: “[Mileage] miles • [gearbox] • [UK location]”
Why it matters: One substantiated specification or rarity fact.
Provenance: “Advert and photos checked [date/time UK]. Seller: [name].”
CTA: “Open the original advert through Gavin’s Picks.”
Footer: “Availability may change. Independently verify condition and history.”

Recheck the actual advert and photographs immediately before posting. Reject POA, sold, reserved, deposit taken, under offer or ambiguous availability. Never automatically turn a search snippet into a social post. Use owned or seller-authorised photos/video for promotional uploads; otherwise use original typography cards. Keep seller attribution and original source links. If sold later, update the caption/pinned comment where supported and remove from the site’s latest finds. Do not promise perpetual availability on a historical social post.
