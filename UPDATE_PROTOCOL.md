## Gavin says editorial notes
Every published listing must include a non-empty `gavinSays` string: 45–85 words in Gavin’s direct, enthusiastic British voice explaining why this particular car earned its place. Lead with its strongest evidenced attraction: gearbox, specification, colour, documented work, ownership or service history. Use first-person judgement and varied openings, not generic sales copy. Attribute unverified seller claims clearly. Only call mileage collector-grade, ownership low or provenance excellent when the evidence supports it; never infer owner character from an owner count. Do not invent inspection or driving experiences. Do not claim past appreciation without dated market evidence, or promise future gains or stronger demand. Keep crucial reservations alongside the relevant appeal. Reassess the copy when evidence, mileage or specification changes; avoid hard-coded prices that can become stale. Preserve `gavinSays` on imports and price updates unless the underlying case changes. Technical checks and fuller notes stay in View verification.
